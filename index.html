About Me

Systems administration, identity and practical IT support.

I’m Mikel Omeibe, an IT professional and Computer Systems Technology student at Fanshawe College in London, Ontario, with expected completion in December 2026. I enjoy understanding how things work, asking questions, and sticking with a problem until I find a way forward. Helping someone feel more confident with technology is one of the most rewarding parts of IT for me.

My experience includes systems administration at Degab Groups in Nigeria and three IT support contract terms with Compass Group North America. I have supported users, troubleshot hardware, software and VPN issues, and worked with Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Entra ID, Group Policy, Conditional Access and Intune. These experiences have strengthened my patience, attention to detail and ability to explain technical issues clearly.

Alongside my work experience, I build practical skills through academic projects in hybrid identity, Azure networking and Linux administration. For our FRANTIC group capstone, my primary contribution was the HR Operations Portal, including Entra ID authentication, role-based access, an Active Directory employee directory and SharePoint leave-request integration.

I’ve earned Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) and am working toward Azure Administrator (AZ-104) and CompTIA Network+. I’m looking for an IT support or systems administration opportunity where I can contribute, take responsibility and keep learning. I bring a dependable work ethic, curiosity and a genuine interest in helping people.
